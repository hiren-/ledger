? Springs — strong stocks that went quiet
The idea engine hunts compression after strength: proven uptrends (relative strength, above the 200-DMA, clean books) whose volatility has contracted — tight ranges, shrinking swings (VCP), drying-up volume. That coiled-spring state is where risk/reward is best. With index.json present, everything is measured from real bars: contraction sequences, base duration, volume signature, shakeout springs. Without it, a CSV proxy mode engages automatically. Spring Score = Strength × Tightness — strength without tightness is chasing; tightness without strength is a trap.

The Today tab — conviction-scored entry decisions

The Today tab now uses a two-stage model: (1) check the market and sector regime, and (2) score each stock on five pillars. Only stocks passing both a bullish regime and a high conviction score get a BUY verdict.

The market regime banner

One banner tells you the health of the market as a whole.

  • Confirmed Uptrend — most stocks above their 50-DMA, new highs outnumber new lows. Normal position sizes.
  • Mild Uptrend — broadly constructive. Standard discipline.
  • Choppy — no clear direction. Reduce size, tighten stops.
  • Defensive — most stocks below 50-DMA. Avoid new entries unless exceptional.

The five pillars (0–100 conviction score)

Trend 25 pts max
Price above SMA50 and SMA200, plus the two Golden Crosses (20/50 and 50/200). Full alignment gets the maximum.
Momentum 25 pts max
Relative strength vs the universe (3M and 6M), RSI in the 45–68 sweet spot, and a bullish MACD cross. This is where modern alpha lives.
Volume 20 pts max
Volume ratio today vs the 30-day average. Above 1.5× is a strong confirmation of institutional interest; 1.1–1.5 is decent.
Volatility setup 15 pts max
Bollinger Band width in the bottom quintile (a "squeeze" — often precedes a large move) and ADX in the 18–30 developing-trend window.
Fundamentals 15 pts max
Piotroski F-Score ≥ 7 and Altman Safe zone. A clean floor so you're never buying a technical setup in a broken business.

Three verdicts

BUY go now
Bullish market regime, conviction ≥ 75, zero red flags, RSI in 35–72. Enter at market price with the stop and target shown.
WAIT set alert
Conviction 55–74, or one specific condition missing. The card shows the exact trigger price or condition to watch.
AVOID skip
Two or more red flags, or conviction below 40. Do not buy.

Entry, Stop, Target, R:R

  • Entry — price to buy at.
  • Stop — 2× ATR below entry (minimum 5%). Sell here, no exceptions.
  • Target — 2× the risk, or 52W high if closer.
  • R:R — risk-to-reward. 2.0 or higher is healthy. Below 1.5, skip.

Freshness chip

  • Fresh (green) — early move, entry window open.
  • Squeeze (violet) — Bollinger contraction; breakout imminent.
  • Extended (amber) — already moved a lot; wait for pullback.
  • Quiet (grey) — range-bound, no rush.
  • Weak (red) — falling, not actionable.
How to use this tab: Check the regime banner. If Defensive, close the tab. If bullish, scan the BUY list, pick one you like, check the conviction score and R:R. If R:R ≥ 2, place limit + stop + target orders. Done. Don't check the price for a week.
What the Today tab does NOT do: predict the future. It doesn't know about earnings, news, or analyst changes. It tells you whether the setup is technically and fundamentally clean enough to enter right now.

Piotroski F-Score & Extended F-Score

The Piotroski F-Score is a 9-point accounting quality check developed by Joseph Piotroski (2000).

  • Positive net income
  • Positive ROA
  • Positive operating cash flow
  • CFO > Net Income
  • Lower long-term debt YoY
  • Higher current ratio YoY
  • No new shares issued
  • Higher gross margin YoY
  • Higher asset turnover YoY

8–9 exceptional · 7 strong · 5–6 mixed · 3–4 weak · 0–2 concern.

Returns: ROE, ROA, ROIC, ROCE, DuPont

ROE — Return on Equity %
Net income / equity. ≥ 15% quality, ≥ 20% excellent, < 10% weak. Pair with DuPont to check leverage.
ROA — Return on Assets %
Net income / total assets. ≥ 8% strong, ≥ 12% exceptional.
ROIC — Return on Invested Capital %
After-tax operating profit / (debt + equity − cash). ≥ 15% quality, ≥ 20% exceptional.
DuPont 3-factor
ROE = Net Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier. A 25% ROE from leverage is not a 25% ROE from margins.

Valuation: P/E, P/B, P/S, PEG, Forward P/E

P/E ×
< 15 cheap · 15–25 fair · > 40 expensive. Pair with PEG.
P/B ×
< 2 reasonable; < 1 below book. Less useful for asset-light.
P/S ×
< 2 reasonable; < 1 cheap. Sector-relative only.
PEG ×
P/E ÷ growth rate. < 1 undervalued; 1–2 fair; > 2 expensive.
EV/EBITDA ×
< 10 cheap; 10–20 fair; > 30 expensive. Capital-structure-neutral.

Risk models: Altman Z, Graham, NCAV

Altman Z-Score Z''
Z'' = 6.56·X1 + 3.26·X2 + 6.72·X3 + 1.05·X4. Z > 2.6 Safe · 1.1–2.6 Grey · < 1.1 Distress.
Graham Number ₹
√(22.5 × EPS × BVPS). MoS > 20% meaningfully undervalued.
NCAV ₹
Current assets − total liabilities. Buy below 0.67× NCAV for deep value.
Net Debt / EBITDA ×
< 2 healthy; 2–4 stretched; > 4 high risk.
Interest Coverage ×
> 5 comfortable; 2–5 adequate; < 2 dangerously thin.

Cash flow & quality: Accruals, Cash Conversion, FCF Yield

Accruals Ratio Sloan 1996
(NI − CFO) / Total assets. Negative is best (cash-backed). > 0.05 warning; > 0.15 serious.
Cash Conversion CFO / NI
> 1.0 good; < 0.7 accrual-driven.
FCF Yield %
> 7% cheap; > 10% very cheap.

Growth & trajectory: FY vs TTM

Comparing TTM growth to FY growth is one of the most under-used patterns in equity screens. When TTM > FY on multiple metrics, the business has inflected since the last reported annual.

The 12 Hidden Gem archetypes

Each archetype is a different way of finding asymmetry.

1. Cash Compounder quality
Negative accruals + cash conversion ≥ 1.1 + ROE not leverage-driven + FCF yield > earnings yield.
2. Quiet Inflection growth
≥ 2 of {EBITDA, FCF, NI, GP} have TTM > FY growth. RSI 45–68.
3. Pre-Breakout technical
Golden 20/50 fired, 50/200 not yet. ADX 18–28 with +DI > −DI. BB squeeze.
4. Sector Orphan relative value
Sector composite > 70, overall score < 75. Best-in-class inside a disliked sector.
5. Forensic Clean forensic
Accruals < 0.02, cash conversion ≥ 0.9, no receivables/inventory bloat.
6. FCF King cash yield
FCF yield > 7%, FCF margin > 12%, net debt/EBITDA < 2.
7. Asset-Light Compounder ROIC
ROIC > 18%, D/E < 0.5, operating margin > 15%.
8. Graham Net-Net deep value
Price / NCAV < 1.0 (ideally < 0.67).
9. Momentum Ignition CANSLIM
6M perf > 10%, 52W position > 55%, TTM growth positive.
10. Buffett Quality quality
ROE > 18%, ROIC > 15%, D/E < 0.5, interest coverage > 8×.
11. Dividend Growth income
Yield 1.5–5%, DPS growth > 8%, payout < 60%, FCF yield > 4%.
12. Working Capital Wizard operations
Receivables/assets < 20%, inventory/assets < 25%, cash conversion > 1.0.

Trap checks & red flags

Every gem card shows a "Trap check" row.

  • Altman — Safe / Grey / Distress.
  • Int.Cov — ≥ 3× healthy; < 2× red flag.
  • NetDebt/EBITDA — < 2× comfortable; > 4× stretched.
  • Accruals — < 0 best; > 0.05 warning; > 0.15 serious.
  • CashConv — ≥ 1.0 good; < 0.7 accrual-driven.

Universal red flags

  • All three ratings Strong Buy — crowded trade.
  • 52W range position > 90% — no margin of safety.
  • Altman Distress.
  • Accruals > 0.10 with cash conversion < 0.7.
  • RSI > 75.
